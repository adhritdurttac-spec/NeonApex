NEON APEX - Arcade Racing
=========================

HOW TO PLAY:
1. Keep index.html and main.js together in this same folder.
2. Double-click index.html (or drag it into your browser).
3. Controls: A/D or Arrow keys = steer, SPACE = nitro, P = pause.

CREDITS: Earn CR by racing (distance, near misses, knockdowns, finish position).
UNLOCK: Click locked cars in the menu to buy them.
TUNING: Select an owned car, then upgrade Engine / Tires / Nitro / Chassis (5 levels each).
Progress saves automatically in your browser.
